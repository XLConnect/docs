# Quickstart

This assumes you have [installed the add-in](../installing-the-add-in.md) and [created an account](getting-started.md). In a few minutes you will open a workbook from the cloud and fill it with live data.

<!-- TODO: add screenshots for each step -->

## 1. Log in

Start Excel and select the `XLConnect` ribbon at the top of the screen. Press the `Login` button — this opens your browser to verify your identity and returns you to Excel. If you were already logged in on the web, the screen just flashes and you're in.

## 2. Open a workbook

On the ribbon, click `Open Workbook`. This drops down a menu with the databases you have access to — hover over a database to see its folders and workbooks, and hover over a workbook to see who last edited it and what it is for.

Click a workbook to open it. If your organisation uses a Start Menu, the `Start Menu` button gives you the same content organized as a launcher.

## 3. Run it

Press `Run` on the ribbon. This executes the scripts in the workbook: XLConnect connects to the cloud, pulls fresh data and lands it in the sheet.

The first time a workbook connects to a cloud system like Xero, a browser window opens so you can log in to that platform and choose which organisation to share. That's a one-time step — the connection is stored securely for next time.

While a script runs you'll see a progress bar telling you what is happening. When it finishes, the workbook's formulas take over and your figures are up to date.

## 4. Start fresh

`Clear Data` removes the loaded data from the workbook, giving you a clean slate to run again — for example after selecting a different company or period.

## That's it

You now know the daily loop: **open → run → read your numbers**. Continue with [Using XLConnect](using-xlconnect.md) for the rest of the daily tasks, or [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) if something didn't work.
