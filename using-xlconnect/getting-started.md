# Getting Started

There are two ways to create an account, by [receiving an invite](getting-started.md#receiving-an-invite) or [creating an account online](getting-started.md#creating-an-account-online).&#x20;

## Receiving an Invite

If you have received an invite to a database by an owner, you can click the green 'Create Account' button at the bottom to take you to the[ XLConnect Login](https://account.xlconnect.net/) page.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt="" width="481"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On the XLConnect Login page, use either your Microsoft or Xero account to sign in. Please note this in itself does not give the app access to any of your information on these platforms, they're only used to vouch for your identity. That way we don't have to store that information, to fit our security strategy.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Invites are tied to an email address. Log in with the address the invite was sent to, or ask the sender to invite you on another address.
{% endhint %}

## Creating an Account Online

You don't need an invite to get started — you can create an account yourself:

1. Go to the [XLConnect Login](https://account.xlconnect.net/) page.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft or Xero account. The first login creates your XLConnect account, with a personal database that only you can access.

The same security note applies: your Microsoft or Xero login is only used to vouch for your identity — it does not give XLConnect access to any of your data on those platforms, and we never see your password.

With your account created, [install the add-in](../installing-the-add-in.md) if you haven't yet, then follow the [Quickstart](quickstart.md) to open your first workbook.
