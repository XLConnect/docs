# Getting Started

There are to way to create an account, by[ receiving an invite](getting-started.md#receiving-an-invite) or [creating an account online](getting-started.md#creating-an-account-online).&#x20;

## Receiving an Invite

If you have received an invite to a database by an owner, you can click the green 'Create Account' button at the bottom to take you to the[ XLConnect Login](https://account.xlconnect.net/) page.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt="" width="481"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On the XLConnect Login page, use either you Microsoft or Xero account to sign in. Please note this in itself does not give the app access to any of your information on these platforms, they're only used to vouch for your identity. That way we don;t have to store that information, to fit our security strategy.&#x20;
